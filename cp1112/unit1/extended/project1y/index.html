Project 1Y — Diverges

This one's meant to feel meaningfully different from the adventure game, not just a fancier version of it. Pick one of the three options below.

Choose One

Option 1 — Small Simulation

A dice-based combat simulator: two fighters trade randomized damage until one falls. Introduces import random.

Option 2 — Data-Driven Twist

Separate your game's data from its logic: a dictionary of dictionaries describing rooms, read by a loop instead of hardcoded text.

Option 3 — Light Class Use

Write a Character class with __init__ and at least one other method — a first look at objects.

Requirements (whichever option you pick)

  1. Clearly working code meeting that option's specific requirements (full detail in the notebook)
  2. A short note explaining why you picked this option
  3. Something that feels meaningfully different from a reskinned Core game — lean into what makes your option distinct

Getting Started

Download the project notebook, save it into your 1_python_learning folder in your cloned repo, and commit once you've saved it.

Download Project_1Y_Diverges.ipynb

Before you submit: run your project top to bottom at least twice — simulations especially, since randomness means one clean run doesn't guarantee the next one won't hit an edge case.
