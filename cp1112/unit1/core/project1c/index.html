Project 1C — Inventory & the Full Game

Checkpoint 3 of 3 — the finish line. Open your Project 1B notebook and keep building from there. Today you add an inventory to your adventure, and then debug a version of your own game with a few deliberately planted bugs before final submission. By the end, you'll have one complete, playable game in your portfolio.

Requirements

  1. Start from your Project 1B code
  2. An inventory list that starts with at least one item
  3. An .append() somewhere in your adventure, and a place the inventory gets printed
  4. Plant 2–3 bugs on purpose in a copy of your working code, then debug them one at a time — same skills as 1.8

Aiming higher? The notebook lists Tier 2 and Tier 3 stretch goals — an inventory that actually affects the story, removing items too, and a short written postmortem on your trickiest bug.

Getting Started

Download the project notebook and paste or rebuild your 1B code into it as your starting point. Save it into your 1_python_learning folder in your cloned repo, and commit once you've saved it.

Download Project_1C_Inventory_and_the_Full_Game.ipynb

Before you submit: run your final, de-bugged version top to bottom at least twice with different choices. This is the version that goes in your portfolio as your Unit 1 deliverable.
