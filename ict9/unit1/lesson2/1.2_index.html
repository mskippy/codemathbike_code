Why the AUA Matters

A few things worth knowing before the agreement goes home for a signature:

  • This isn't just a permission slip — it covers two things: how you use school tech generally, and how you use AI specifically.
  • AI is allowed and expected in this course, like at a real studio — but it has to be documented, never hidden.
  • You'll keep a running AI log all semester: every time you use AI, note what you asked, what you got back, what you changed, and what you learned.
  • Take it home, read it with a parent/guardian, and bring the signed copy back — you can always ask questions before then.

Task 4 — Word Skills: Class Directory

Complete the Class Directory document to the best of your ability. Ask the people around you and/or search what you don't know. Make your answers stand out.

1. Get to know the people around you.

Our stations sit in rows — three computers on each side of the room, with an aisle down the middle. "Around you" means your row (both sides) plus whoever's directly in front of or behind you — not across the aisle. Talk to each person and learn one interesting thing about them.

2. Folder check (reminder).

Confirm your OneDrive → ICT 9 folders are still set up from Task 2 — and that the same structure exists on your H: drive.

3. Create a table.

  1. Insert a 1-row table with one column for each person you talked to, plus one for yourself.
  2. Change the font for the table to any font that looks like handwriting.
  3. Make the font size 16.
  4. Put the following title above your table: "ICT 9 Class." Underline and bold the title.
  5. In the appropriate spots, write the name of each person you talked to and the interesting thing you learned about them.
  6. Include yourself in the table, and change the font colour for your own information.

4. Save and submit.

Save this document in OneDrive → ICT 9 → Unit 1, titled 1.2_Class_Directory_[YourName]. In MS Teams, find the Lesson 1.2 assignment and attach your file.

Task 5 — Get to Know You

A short Word doc you build yourself, same pattern as Task 4 — a couple of these double as quick reminders of skills you've already got.

Field Requirement
File name1.2_Get_to_Know_You_[YourName]
Save locationOneDrive → ICT 9 → Unit 1
ContentsAbout You answers + one cited image + one folder screenshot, all in the same doc

About You

  • What should I call you? (preferred name / nickname)
  • Pronouns (optional)
  • One thing you're looking forward to this year — or honestly dreading.
  • Games you play or enjoy watching (any genre) — helps with team-building later in the semester.
  • What devices do you use most (laptop, phone, console, etc.) and for what?
  • What's your dream job — even a silly one?
  • How comfortable are you with computers/tech right now? (1–5 scale)
  • What's something you'd like to learn in this course?
  • Anything I should know to help you succeed this semester? (optional, private — only you see it)

Add an Image (reminder: sourcing + citing)

Find an image online that represents one of your interests (favourite place, album cover, sports team) — not a photo of yourself. Paste it into the doc.

  • Underneath the image, note where you found it (the site name). That's your citation — same habit you'll need all semester whenever you use someone else's work.

Folder Screenshot (reminder: proof Task 2 actually worked)

Take a screenshot (Windows + Shift + S) of OneDrive → ICT 9 → Unit 1, showing your Class Directory file inside, and paste it into this same doc.
