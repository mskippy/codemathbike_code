1.1 Welcome to the Studio

Unit 1: IT Onboarding   Day 1 of 3

Welcome to the studio — starting today, you're a new hire. This class runs like a small game studio with four departments, and Unit 1 is your onboarding: getting set up, meeting the team, and learning how we work before you build anything.

The Four Departments

  • IT Onboarding (now) — getting set up, meeting the team
  • Web Dept — building the studio's online presence
  • Dev Team — designing and shipping a project: a game, app, or interactive story, your choice
  • Innovation Lab — what else the studio can build, and our responsibility as tech creators

Bookmark the course site now: code.codemathbike.ca/ict9. Every lesson and resource lives here all semester, so you'll never fall behind after being away.

Employee Handbook — Our Three Expectations

Every employee here operates under three expectations.

Respect

For the space — shared equipment and workstations, left ready for the next person. For people — this is a team, not solo work in a room full of strangers. For yourself — do work you'd actually put your name on.

Accountability

You get real independence in this class. Most days are work time, not lecture. That independence only works if you show up ready to use it: deadlines, work time, and self-management are on you.

Independence

Troubleshoot first — course site, your notes, a neighbour — before flagging your teacher down. They're here to coach, not to hand-hold every step.

Get Set Up

  1. Log into your Windows PC.
  2. Find and bookmark the course site: code.codemathbike.ca/ict9.
  3. Locate or create your student folder for the semester.
  4. Join the class Team, and post a test message in the intro channel to confirm your access.

Today's Task — New Hire ID Card

Every new hire gets an ID card. This is your first hands-on tool (Canva) and your first artifact for the studio wall — a get-to-know-you activity, not a graded assignment.

Due: start of next class (Lesson 1.2). Finishing it tonight is your first small test of the Accountability expectation above.

Tool: Canva (school account)

Required Fields

Field Notes
Name
PhotoHeadshot, or a school-appropriate avatar if you don't have a photo
DepartmentWhich studio department (Web Dept / Dev Team / Innovation Lab) you're most excited to join
Skill I'm bringingOne line — a skill, interest, or strength you bring to the studio
Employee ID #Optional fun touch — e.g. the last 4 digits of your student number
Start DateToday's date — reinforces the "new hire" framing

Steps

  1. Log into Canva.
  2. Search for an "Employee ID Badge" or "ID Card" template — or use the template your teacher shares — and pick something clean and professional.
  3. Fill in all the required fields above.
  4. Swap in your photo or avatar.
  5. Export as PNG or PDF.
  6. Submit to the Teams assignment by the start of next class.

Wrap-Up & What's Next

Your ID card is due at the start of Lesson 1.2 — printed copies go up on the wall as the studio's "meet the team" gallery.

Day 2: finishing your folder setup, the Typing Club baseline test, signing the Acceptable Use & AI Use Agreement, and a quick Get to Know You survey.

Day 3 wraps onboarding with a group icebreaker before our regular weekly rhythm kicks in — the real content (computers & binary) starts in Week 2.
