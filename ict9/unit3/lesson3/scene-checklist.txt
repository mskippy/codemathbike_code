POLISH CHECKLIST
=================
After all scenes are built, go through this checklist:

Content
  ☐ No placeholder text or graphics remain
  ☐ All scenes have clear start and end points
  ☐ Transitions between scenes are smooth (not jarring)
  ☐ HUD and UI consistent across all scenes

Functionality
  ☐ Each scene is playable end-to-end with no crashes
  ☐ No soft locks (player can't proceed)
  ☐ Win/lose/next conditions work as designed
  ☐ Save/reload works if applicable

Art & Audio
  ☐ All sprites match the team's art style and color palette
  ☐ Fonts and text sizes consistent
  ☐ Audio (if used) doesn't overlap or cut out

Difficulty & Balance
  ☐ Progression feels right (not too hard, not too easy)
  ☐ First scene is a good intro; later scenes build on it
  ☐ No unfair instant-deaths or confusion
