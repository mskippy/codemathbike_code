Unit 2 — Digital Media

In this unit, you’ll learn how to use Canva and simple photo-editing tools to create digital work that is organized, clear, and visually appealing.

What this unit is about

Digital media is everywhere — in school, at home, and online. This unit focuses on a few core tools and skills so you can build projects you’re proud of without feeling overwhelmed.

What you’ll learn

  • Basics of design principles (contrast, alignment, spacing, hierarchy)
  • How to create clean, readable layouts in Canva
  • How to remove or edit unwanted elements in a photo
  • How to combine design and photo-editing skills into one finished piece
  • How to reflect on your work and explain your creative choices
