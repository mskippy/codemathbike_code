Troubleshooting Quick Reference

  • Monitor won't turn on: check the power cable at both ends; check the monitor's own power button (separate from the tower); check whether the tower itself is on (fan noise, lights).
  • Computer is frozen: try Ctrl+Alt+Delete first to close the stuck program; if that doesn't work, hold the power button 5–10 seconds to force a restart, then turn it back on.
  • When to ask for help: after trying the steps above, or any time you're unsure whether something is safe to try — ask a teacher right away rather than keep experimenting.
