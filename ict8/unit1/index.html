Welcome to Unit 1

In this unit, you’ll get connected and build the everyday digital habits you’ll rely on all year: communicating professionally, navigating Teams and OneDrive, saving and organizing your files, and understanding the computer you’re working on. By the end, you’ll feel confident logging in, staying organized, and troubleshooting simple problems on your own.

Learning Goals

  • I can communicate professionally by email and navigate our Teams class.
  • I can save, rename, and organize my files in the right place — including files I download or receive through Teams.
  • I can take a screenshot and use it as proof of my work.
  • I can identify the parts of a computer and know basic troubleshooting steps.

Unit Objectives

  • Set up and navigate a school email account and Microsoft Teams.
  • Save, rename, and organize files and folders in OneDrive.
  • Take screenshots and submit multi-file assignments through Teams.
  • Identify computer hardware parts and apply basic troubleshooting steps.
