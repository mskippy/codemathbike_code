Course Reflection

This course, I learnt a lot. I learnt about the skills to make a video game, how to make a website (using a template), and lots of other things. I also gained more skills in typing, and using canva and photo-editing. I enjoyed using the photo editing techniques with canvas magic eraser, and I know I'll use that in the future. One of my favourite parts of ICT 8 was making my video game. I really liked getting to play it and learning how to code a video game properly. Another thing I enjoyed was the ability to be independent in class, only having to listen a little bit. I get tired listening to talking and lessons all day, but this class was different because we actually followed stuff (reading instructions) and not talking as much. Overall, I really liked my 4 weeks in ICT 8.
