Course Reflection

Essential Skills

In Essential Skills, I enjoyed making the folders in my OneDrive. What I found most challenging was the posters assignment, as it was very time consuming; I will definitely use those skills in the future, whether its in school or for work.

Digital Media

In the Digital Media unit, I enjoyed making the Canva about me posters & editing images. I found the Excel project the hardest, as I didn't want to ask everyone some random questions. I would like to learn more about the other Microsoft apps like Powerpoint, OneNote, etc. For what I will use in the future, I think I will utilize everything I learned, as it is a core part of digital skills..

Game Development

During Game Development, what I found the most enjoyable was customizing my game. If we had more time, I would love to learn more about games and how to make different genres instead of the standard shooter-style game we made. What I found the most challenging was doing some parts of the coding, as sometimes the intstructions were very vague and I had to figure it out by myself. I probably won't use this skill in the future.

Web Development

In the Web Development, I enjoyed making this website. What I found most difficult was adding the image above, as you couldn't use images directly from Pinterest, only from the Google searches. This made it harder to find pleasing images. I'd like to learn more about making more features, like more pages, interactive buttons, etc. I might use this in the future.
